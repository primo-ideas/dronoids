The game

A space without edges

A million units across, generated as it is discovered: asteroid belts, empty stretches, crystal fields richer the further they are from anything.

You steer nothing

Your dronoids follow a program. The better it is written, the better they mine, explore and defend the colony. The programs provided are naive on purpose.

A world that goes on

The sector is shared and persistent: it runs day and night, other colonies settle in it, and yours keep working while you are away.

The language

DASM fits on one page: four registers, sensors, jumps, and actions that take time. A dronoid runs its instructions up to an action, then starts over.

The starting program, miner, goes to the nearest crystal, digs it, then goes home to the factory. Up to you to do better.

; MINER -- the starting program
start:
    CARGO a
    JGE a, FULL, home
    DIST a, ORE
    JEQ a, NONE, wander
    JLE a, 1, dig
    SCAN b, ORE
    MOVE b
    JMP start
dig:
    MINE
    JMP start

Your account

An account keeps your nickname and your colony, on all your devices. All that is kept is your nickname and the identifier the chosen service gives us: no email address, no name.

Signing in with Google, Discord, GitHub or Twitch opens very soon. Until then, the game stays open to everyone, without an account.

One last step: the nickname the other colonies will see you under. It cannot be changed afterwards.
